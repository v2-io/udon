The sensor recorded a speed of 3.5 × 10⁸ m/s at a temperature of 21 °C.

The same sentence in LaTeX with `siunitx`:

```latex
The sensor recorded a speed of \SI{3.5e8}{\meter\per\second} at a temperature of \SI{21}{\celsius}.
```

Typesetting notes:
- A (thin) non-breaking space separates each number from its unit.
- The degree sign and "C" are written as `°C`, with no space between them.
- The unit "m/s" is upright (roman), not italic.
- The multiplication sign is `×`, not the letter "x".

Also, 3.5 × 10⁸ m/s is faster than the speed of light (about 2.998 × 10⁸ m/s), so you may want a different value if the sentence describes something physical.
