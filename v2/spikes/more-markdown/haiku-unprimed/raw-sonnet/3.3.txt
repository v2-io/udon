Here's a sentence using a non-breaking space (U+00A0) between "Dr." and "Smith" so they stay together on one line:

The committee asked Dr. Smith to present the findings at the annual conference.

The space between "Dr." and "Smith" is a non-breaking space, which prevents a line wrap from occurring there.

For other contexts:

- **HTML:** `Dr.&nbsp;Smith`
- **LaTeX:** `Dr.~Smith`
- **Markdown/plain text:** use the literal U+00A0 character
