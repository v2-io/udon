Here's an example of a nested quoted reply in standard email/Markdown style:

```
On Mon, Oct 12, 2026, Priya Natarajan wrote:
> On Fri, Oct 9, 2026, Joseph Wecker wrote:
> > Hi Priya,
> >
> > Could we move the design review to Thursday afternoon?
> > I'd like to give the team time to finish the prototype first.
> >
> > Thanks,
> > Joseph
>
> Hi Joseph,
>
> Thursday afternoon works for me. I'll update the invite
> and let the rest of the group know.
>
> Best,
> Priya

Thanks Priya, that's perfect. See everyone Thursday.

Joseph
```

And here is how it renders in Markdown:

On Mon, Oct 12, 2026, Priya Natarajan wrote:
> On Fri, Oct 9, 2026, Joseph Wecker wrote:
> > Hi Priya,
> >
> > Could we move the design review to Thursday afternoon?
> > I'd like to give the team time to finish the prototype first.
> >
> > Thanks,
> > Joseph
>
> Hi Joseph,
>
> Thursday afternoon works for me. I'll update the invite and let the rest of the group know.
>
> Best,
> Priya

Thanks Priya, that's perfect. See everyone Thursday.

Joseph

Each additional level of reply adds another `>` prefix, so the earliest message sits at the deepest nesting level.
