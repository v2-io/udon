# The Long Way Home

## Part One: Departure

### Chapter 1: The Letter

The envelope arrived on a gray Tuesday, bearing no return address and a handwriting Mara had not seen in twenty years.

### Chapter 2: The Packing

By nightfall she had filled a single suitcase, leaving behind everything except the letter and the key that had fallen out of it.

---

## Part Two: Arrival

### Chapter 3: The Station

The train let her off at a platform so small it had no name, only a rusted lamp swinging in the wind.

### Chapter 4: The House

At the end of the lane stood the house from her childhood, and the key turned in the lock as though it had been waiting for her all along.
