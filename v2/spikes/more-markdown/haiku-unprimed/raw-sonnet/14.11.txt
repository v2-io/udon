Here's a short LaTeX outline with an automatically generated list of figures:

```latex
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{graphicx}

\title{Project Report Outline}
\author{Joseph Wecker}
\date{\today}

\begin{document}

\maketitle
\tableofcontents
\listoffigures
\newpage

\section{Introduction}
Background, motivation, and goals.

\section{Methods}
Describe the approach.

\section{Discussion}
Interpretation and limitations.

\section{Conclusion}
Key takeaways and next steps.

\end{document}
```

**How it works**
- `\listoffigures` builds the list from every `\caption{...}` inside a `figure` environment, including page numbers.
- Compile twice (`pdflatex file.tex` two times) so the list and cross-references resolve.
- Replace `example-image` with your actual image files.
