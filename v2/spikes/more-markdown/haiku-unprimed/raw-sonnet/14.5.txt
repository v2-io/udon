# Chapter Seven
## The Lighthouse Keeper's Ledger

> *"A light is only a promise that someone, somewhere, is still awake."*
>
> — Marguerite Aldous, *Letters from the Outer Skerries*

---

The lamp on Harrow Rock had not failed in a hundred and eleven years, and Tobias Venn did not intend to be the man who broke the streak.

He climbed the last of the stairs at dusk, as he did every evening, counting them under his breath. There were two hundred and four, and the one hundred and ninth still creaked. Wind pressed against the lantern room glass, and beyond it the sea was turning from pewter to ink. Somewhere out past the reef, a ship he would never see was steering by the light he was about to kindle.

He struck the match. The flame caught, shivered, and steadied.

It was only later, with the beam sweeping its slow circle over the water and the kettle muttering on the stove below, that he opened the ledger and noticed the entry that was not in his own handwriting.
