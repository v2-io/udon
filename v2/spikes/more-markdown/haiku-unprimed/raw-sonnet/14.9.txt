# Side-by-Side Figure with Sub-captions

## HTML

```html
<p>
  As shown in Figure 1, the model's output differs noticeably before and after
  fine-tuning. The untuned model produces noisy, fragmented results (Figure 1a),
  whereas the fine-tuned model yields cleaner and more coherent output (Figure 1b).
</p>

<figure style="text-align:center; margin:1.5em auto; max-width:900px;">
  <div style="display:flex; justify-content:center; gap:1.5em;">
    <figure style="margin:0; flex:1;">
      <img src="image_a.png" alt="Before fine-tuning" style="width:100%;">
      <figcaption>(a) Before fine-tuning</figcaption>
    </figure>
    <figure style="margin:0; flex:1;">
      <img src="image_b.png" alt="After fine-tuning" style="width:100%;">
      <figcaption>(b) After fine-tuning</figcaption>
    </figure>
  </div>
  <figcaption style="margin-top:0.75em;">
    <strong>Figure 1:</strong> Comparison of model output before and after fine-tuning.
  </figcaption>
</figure>
```

## LaTeX

```latex
\usepackage{subcaption} % in the preamble
\usepackage{graphicx}

As shown in Figure~\ref{fig:comparison}, the model's output differs noticeably
before and after fine-tuning. The untuned model produces noisy, fragmented
results (Figure~\ref{fig:before}), whereas the fine-tuned model yields cleaner
and more coherent output (Figure~\ref{fig:after}).

\begin{figure}[htbp]
  \centering
  \begin{subfigure}[b]{0.48\textwidth}
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{image_a.png}
    \caption{Before fine-tuning}
    \label{fig:before}
  \end{subfigure}
  \hfill
  \begin{subfigure}[b]{0.48\textwidth}
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{image_b.png}
    \caption{After fine-tuning}
    \label{fig:after}
  \end{subfigure}
  \caption{Comparison of model output before and after fine-tuning.}
  \label{fig:comparison}
\end{figure}
```

## Markdown (HTML table approach)

```markdown
<table>
  <tr>
    <td align="center"><img src="image_a.png" width="300"><br>(a) Before fine-tuning</td>
    <td align="center"><img src="image_b.png" width="300"><br>(b) After fine-tuning</td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td colspan="2" align="center"><b>Figure 1:</b> Comparison of model output before and after fine-tuning.</td>
  </tr>
</table>
```

Replace `image_a.png` and `image_b.png` with your image paths, and adjust the captions and passage text to fit your content. If you need a different format (Word, Typst, reStructuredText, etc.), let me know.
