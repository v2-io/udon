# Miso the Window-Watcher

Miso is a small, cream-colored cat with a caramel patch over one eye, which is how she got her name: she looks like a bowl of soup with a dollop of paste on top.

Every morning, Miso takes up her post on the living room windowsill. From there she supervises the neighborhood: the birds on the fence, the mail carrier, and the occasional squirrel who dares to cross the lawn. She tracks them all with unblinking seriousness, tail twitching at the tip.

By afternoon, her duties are done. She curls into a perfect circle on the warmest patch of sunlight and sleeps until dinner, at which point she announces, loudly and repeatedly, that it is time.

At night, Miso trades her post for the foot of the bed, where she purrs like a tiny engine until everyone is asleep.
