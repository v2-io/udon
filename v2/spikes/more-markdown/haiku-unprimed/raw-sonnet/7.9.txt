# The Quiet Hour Before Dawn

</div>

There is a particular stillness that settles over the world in the hour before sunrise, when the streets are empty, the air is cool and unhurried, and the first pale light begins to seep along the horizon. It is a time that belongs to no one in particular, neither the end of the night nor quite the start of the day, and in that gap the mind feels unusually clear. Thoughts that were tangled the evening before loosen and sort themselves, and small sounds, like a distant bird or the hum of a kettle, take on a gentle significance. Those who wake early enough to notice it often find that the hour offers a kind of unspoken promise: that whatever the day brings, it begins in calm.
