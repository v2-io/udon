Plain text and Markdown have no native "include" feature, so the simplest approach is to build the document by concatenating `intro.txt` with the body.

**1. Body file (`body.md`)**

```markdown

## Overview

This document builds on the introduction above. It summarizes the key points
and outlines next steps.

## Key Points

- The introduction sets the context.
- This section adds supporting detail.
- Next steps are listed below.

## Next Steps

1. Review the material.
2. Share feedback.
3. Finalize and publish.
```

**2. Build the document**

```sh
cat intro.txt body.md > document.md
```

This puts the full contents of `intro.txt` at the top, followed by the body.

**Alternatives, depending on your tooling**

- **Pandoc / Markdown with includes:** use a filter such as `pandoc-include` and write `` ```{.include}
intro.txt
``` `` at the top of the file.
- **LaTeX:** `\input{intro.txt}` at the top of the document.
- **AsciiDoc:** `include::intro.txt[]` at the top.
