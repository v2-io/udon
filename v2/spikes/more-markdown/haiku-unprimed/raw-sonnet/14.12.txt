# Remote Work Guidelines

## Section 1: Communication Expectations

Clear communication keeps distributed teams aligned. Team members should:

- **Respond promptly.** Acknowledge direct messages within four working hours, even if only to say a fuller reply is coming.
- **Default to written updates.** Share progress in the team channel so decisions and context are visible to everyone, including those in other time zones.
- **Use video selectively.** Reserve meetings for discussions that benefit from real-time interaction, such as brainstorming, sensitive feedback, or resolving disagreements.
- **State availability.** Keep calendars and status indicators current so colleagues know when to expect responses.

## Section 2: Workspace and Wellbeing

A sustainable remote setup supports both productivity and health. Team members are encouraged to:

- **Set boundaries.** Define working hours and communicate them to the team. Log off at the end of the day.
- **Take regular breaks.** Step away from the screen at least once every 90 minutes.
- **Maintain a dedicated space.** Where possible, work from a consistent, comfortable area with good lighting and ergonomic seating.
- **Stay connected.** Schedule occasional informal check-ins or virtual coffee chats to maintain team relationships.
