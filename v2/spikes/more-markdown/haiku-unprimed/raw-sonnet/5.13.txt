Here is a short paragraph with a margin note in LaTeX, using `\marginpar` so the note sits beside the text instead of at the bottom of the page:

```latex
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage[a4paper,margin=1in,marginparwidth=1.5in]{geometry}

\begin{document}

Printing presses changed how ideas travelled across Europe. Where a scribe
might produce a single volume in months, a workshop could now turn out
hundreds of copies in a fortnight.%
\marginpar{\footnotesize Gutenberg's press, c.\,1450, made movable-type
printing practical in Europe.}
The effect was a steady drop in the price of books and a matching rise in
literacy, which in turn fed the demand for still more printed work.

\end{document}
```

**Notes:**
- `\marginpar{...}` places the note in the margin next to the line where it appears. `\footnotesize` keeps it from crowding the page.
- `marginparwidth` in the `geometry` options sets the margin's width, so widen it if your notes are longer.
- If you need notes that float cleanly at exact positions (or in a two-sided layout), the `marginnote` or `sidenotes` packages are more robust than `\marginpar`.
