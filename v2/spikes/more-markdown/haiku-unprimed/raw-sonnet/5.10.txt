# A Day in Kyoto

We arrived in Kyoto early in the morning, when the streets were still quiet and the air was cool. Fushimi Inari was our first stop: thousands of vermilion torii gates winding up the mountain, with sunlight filtering through in strips. By midday we'd wandered into Gion, where we found a small tea house tucked down a side street and sampled matcha with wagashi, delicate seasonal sweets.

The afternoon was spent at Kinkaku-ji, the Golden Pavilion, shimmering over its mirror-like pond. We ended the day with a slow walk along the Philosopher's Path as the lanterns came on, feeling like we'd stepped out of time.
