# Getting Started with Your Project

Setting up a new project doesn't have to be complicated. Start by creating a clear folder structure, initializing version control, and documenting your goals in a short README.

> 💡 **Tip**
> Commit early and often. Small, focused commits make it much easier to track down bugs and roll back changes when something goes wrong.

Once your foundation is in place, you can begin building out features one piece at a time.
