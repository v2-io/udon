# The Value of Small Habits

## Introduction

Small habits are the quiet engines of long-term change. Rather than relying on bursts of motivation, they build progress through consistent, manageable actions repeated over time. This document outlines why small habits matter and how to put them into practice.

## Building Habits That Last

Lasting habits come from making new behaviors easy to start and easy to repeat. The goal is not perfection but consistency, so that the habit becomes part of your routine without requiring much willpower.

### Practical Steps

1. **Start tiny.** Choose an action that takes under two minutes, such as writing one sentence or doing five push-ups.
2. **Anchor it to an existing routine.** Attach the new habit to something you already do, like brewing morning coffee.
3. **Track your progress.** A simple checkmark on a calendar provides a visible reward and reinforces the streak.
4. **Adjust gradually.** Once the habit feels automatic, increase the difficulty or duration slightly.
