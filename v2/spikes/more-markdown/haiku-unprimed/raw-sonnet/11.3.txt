Here's a passage with the video embedded inline using the HTML5 `<video>` element:

```html
<section>
  <h2>Watch the Talk</h2>
  <p>
    In this session, the speaker walks through the key ideas and takeaways
    from the presentation. Press play below to watch it right here on the page,
    no download required.
  </p>

  <video controls preload="metadata" width="720" style="max-width: 100%;">
    <source src="talk.mp4" type="video/mp4">
    Your browser doesn't support inline video playback.
    <a href="talk.mp4">Download the talk (MP4)</a>.
  </video>

  <p>
    If the video doesn't load, make sure <code>talk.mp4</code> is in the same
    folder as this page.
  </p>
</section>
```

**Notes:**
- `controls` gives the viewer play, pause, seek, and volume controls.
- `preload="metadata"` loads only the duration and dimensions until the user presses play.
- The `style="max-width: 100%;"` keeps the video responsive on small screens.
- The text inside `<video>` is a fallback for browsers that can't play it.
- On iOS, add `playsinline` to the `<video>` tag if you want it to play within the page rather than going fullscreen.
