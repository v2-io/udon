Here's a version in JavaScript where the product name is defined once as a constant and interpolated into each sentence:

```js
const PRODUCT_NAME = "Acme Widget";

const sentences = [
  `${PRODUCT_NAME} makes everyday tasks faster and simpler.`,
  `Thousands of teams rely on ${PRODUCT_NAME} to stay organized.`,
  `Try ${PRODUCT_NAME} today and see the difference for yourself.`,
];

console.log(sentences.join(" "));
```

**Output:**

> Acme Widget makes everyday tasks faster and simpler. Thousands of teams rely on Acme Widget to stay organized. Try Acme Widget today and see the difference for yourself.

To rename the product, change only the `PRODUCT_NAME` value on the first line.

If you'd prefer plain text with a placeholder, here's the same idea as a template:

> **{{PRODUCT_NAME}}** makes everyday tasks faster and simpler. Thousands of teams rely on **{{PRODUCT_NAME}}** to stay organized. Try **{{PRODUCT_NAME}}** today and see the difference for yourself.

with `PRODUCT_NAME = Acme Widget` defined once at the top.
