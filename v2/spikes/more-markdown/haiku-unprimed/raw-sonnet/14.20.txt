Here is a contract clause with three paragraphs that number themselves 1.1, 1.2 and 1.3 using CSS counters. If you add, remove, or reorder paragraphs, the numbers update on their own.

```html
<!DOCTYPE html>
<html>
<head>
<style>
  body { counter-reset: clause; font-family: Georgia, serif; max-width: 40em; margin: 2em auto; }
  h2 { counter-increment: clause; counter-reset: sub; }
  h2::before { content: counter(clause) ". "; }
  p.numbered { counter-increment: sub; padding-left: 3em; text-indent: -3em; }
  p.numbered::before { content: counter(clause) "." counter(sub) "\00a0\00a0\00a0"; font-weight: bold; }
</style>
</head>
<body>

<h2>Definitions and Interpretation</h2>

<p class="numbered">
  In this Agreement, "Services" means the services described in Schedule A, to be
  provided by the Supplier to the Client in accordance with the terms set out herein.
</p>

<p class="numbered">
  "Effective Date" means the date on which this Agreement has been signed by both
  Parties, and the Agreement shall remain in force until terminated in accordance
  with its provisions.
</p>

<p class="numbered">
  Headings are included for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation
  of this Agreement. References to clauses and schedules are to clauses of and
  schedules to this Agreement.
</p>

</body>
</html>
```

**Rendered result:**

> **1. Definitions and Interpretation**
>
> **1.1** In this Agreement, "Services" means the services described in Schedule A, to be provided by the Supplier to the Client in accordance with the terms set out herein.
>
> **1.2** "Effective Date" means the date on which this Agreement has been signed by both Parties, and the Agreement shall remain in force until terminated in accordance with its provisions.
>
> **1.3** Headings are included for convenience only and shall not affect the interpretation of this Agreement. References to clauses and schedules are to clauses of and schedules to this Agreement.
