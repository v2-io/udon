**The Quiet Return of the Paper Map**

In an age of turn-by-turn directions, the paper map has quietly become something other than a tool. Unfolding one on a café table means committing to a picture of the whole terrain at once, something no phone screen allows. Its creases hold the memory of every past trip, and its margins hold the pencil marks of people who got lost and found their way back. Navigation apps promise that we will never be lost again, but the paper map suggests that being lost was never the real problem. The real problem was never knowing where we stood in relation to everything else. **"A map doesn't tell you where to go. It tells you where you are."**

> # "A map doesn't tell you where to go. It tells you where you are."
