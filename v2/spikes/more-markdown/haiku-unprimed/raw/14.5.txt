# Chapter One

*We are such stuff*
*As dreams are made on, and our little life*
*Is rounded with a sleep.*

— William Shakespeare, *The Tempest*, Act IV, Scene 1

The house on Alder Street had been empty for eleven days when Nora finally found the key. It was not where the realtor had said it would be, taped beneath the mailbox, but inside a coffee tin on the kitchen windowsill, wrapped in a square of yellowed cloth. Someone had left it there on purpose, and that was the first thing that made her uneasy. The second was that the tin was still warm from the sun, though the glass had been dark and the kitchen cold since morning.
