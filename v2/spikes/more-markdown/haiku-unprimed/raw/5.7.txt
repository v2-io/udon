Our results show a clear upward trend over the observation period, as summarized in Figure~\ref{fig:chart}.

\begin{figure}[h]
    \centering
    \includegraphics[width=0.8\textwidth]{chart.png}

The `\ref{fig:chart}` command inserts the figure number LaTeX assigns automatically, so the number updates if you add or move figures. Compile with `pdflatex` (or your preferred LaTeX engine) from the directory containing `chart.png`.
