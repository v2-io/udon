Markdown tables can't hold real multi-line cells, so this uses `<br>` tags for line breaks and `•` characters for the bullets:

| Feature | Details |
|---|---|
| Bulleted list | • First item<br>• Second item<br>• Third item |
| Two lines of text | This is the first line.<br>This is the second line. |

If your renderer supports HTML, you can use real list markup instead:

```html
<table>
  <tr>
    <th>Feature</th>
    <th>Details</th>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td>
      <ul>
        <li>First item</li>
        <li>Second item</li>
        <li>Third item</li>
      </ul>
    </td>
    <td>This is the first line.<br>This is the second line.</td>
  </tr>
</table>
```
