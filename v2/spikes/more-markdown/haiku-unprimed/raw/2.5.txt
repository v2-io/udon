The best time to start a habit is usually today, not some imagined future day when your schedule is calmer. Small, specific actions work better than broad resolutions. Instead of promising to "exercise more," commit to a ten-minute walk after lunch. Once that becomes automatic, you can build on it, and consistency matters far more than intensity in the early weeks.

---

Progress also depends on how you measure it. Tracking a single habit on a calendar, with a simple mark for each day you complete it, makes the effort visible and gives you a reason to keep the streak going. Missing a day is not a failure, but skipping two in a row is worth noticing. Treat each lapse as information about what got in the way, and adjust the plan so it's easier to repeat the next day.
