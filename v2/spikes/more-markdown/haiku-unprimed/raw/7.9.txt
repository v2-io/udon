<p align="center"><strong>The Quiet Power of Small Habits</strong></p>

Small habits often look insignificant on any given day, yet their effects compound over time. A few minutes of reading, a short walk, or a simple daily plan can shape who we become far more than occasional bursts of effort. The key is consistency rather than intensity: when a habit is easy enough to repeat, it becomes part of our routine, and the progress that follows is steady and lasting.
