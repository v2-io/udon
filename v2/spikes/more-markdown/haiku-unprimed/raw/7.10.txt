Here is a short paragraph about the value of plain-text notes for long-running projects, followed by LaTeX that will only render in the PDF output.

Keeping notes in plain text makes them easy to search, version, and carry between tools. A project log that records decisions, open questions, and dated observations becomes a reliable record that outlasts any single editor or device. Writing entries in complete sentences, rather than fragments, also tends to clarify thinking, because the act of explaining a decision often reveals gaps in the reasoning behind it.
