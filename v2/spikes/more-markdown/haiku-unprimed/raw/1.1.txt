# The Adventures of Miso

Miso is a small orange tabby with a white bib and eyes the color of warm honey. She came into the world on a rainy spring morning and spent her first months hiding in the laundry basket, emerging only for meals and the occasional sunbeam.

Now grown, Miso has claimed the windowsill above the kitchen sink as her personal kingdom. Each morning she watches the birds at the feeder with the seriousness of a general surveying a battlefield. Her favorite pastime is batting at loose hair ties and pretending she never wanted them in the first place.

She greets every guest at the door, not out of friendliness, but to make sure they know who owns the house. Once they sit down, she will often choose the warmest lap in the room and settle in with a contented purr that rumbles like a distant engine.

Miso may be small, but she runs the household with quiet confidence, and everyone who lives with her knows exactly who is in charge.
