Here's some text with an `experiment` admonition in MkDocs syntax:

```markdown
We're testing a new caching layer to see whether it reduces page load times.

!!! experiment "Caching trial"
    This feature is experimental. Results may change, and it isn't yet recommended for production use. Feedback is welcome.

Once the trial is complete, we'll decide whether to keep the caching layer.
```

For reStructuredText (Sphinx), you could define a custom admonition with `.. admonition::`:

```rst
We're testing a new caching layer to see whether it reduces page load times.

.. admonition:: Caching trial
   :class: experiment

   This feature is experimental. Results may change, and it isn't yet recommended for production use. Feedback is welcome.

Once the trial is complete, we'll decide whether to keep the caching layer.
```
