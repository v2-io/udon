**Kyoto Trip Notes**

Spent a few days in Kyoto, and it lived up to the hype. Early mornings at Fushimi Inari were worth the effort: the torii gates were nearly empty before the crowds arrived. Kiyomizu-dera at sunset was another highlight, and the wooden stage overlooking the trees was stunning in the fading light. I also wandered the Gion district in the evening, hoping for a glimpse of a geiko or maiko, and kept my distance out of respect for their privacy.

Tips for next time:
- Book temple and garden tickets ahead when you can.
- Use the bus system, or walk, since the city is compact.
- Try a kaiseki meal at least once.
- Leave room in the schedule for the small side streets, which were some of my favorite moments.
