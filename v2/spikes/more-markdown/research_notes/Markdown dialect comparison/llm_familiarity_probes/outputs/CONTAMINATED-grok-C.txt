```markdown
# Cooking udon

Boil thick wheat noodles, warm a light broth, and eat the bowl while it is hot.

> [!warning]
> The boiling water foams hard once the noodles go in. Leave headroom in the pot.

**dashi.** A clear Japanese stock simmered from kombu and shaved katsuobushi.[^1]

| Piece | Aim |
| --- | --- |
| Noodles | Springy, with a faint bite |
| Broth | Hot, light, and savory |
| Finish | Scallion and a few drops of soy |

Serve the moment the noodles look ==glossy and bouncy==.

Related note: [[soy-tare]].

[^1]: Kombu is dried kelp; katsuobushi is smoked, dried bonito flakes.
```
