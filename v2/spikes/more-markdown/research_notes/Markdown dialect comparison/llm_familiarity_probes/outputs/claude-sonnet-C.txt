```markdown
# Cooking Udon

Udon are thick wheat noodles, best served in a hot broth with **==fresh scallions==** on top.

> [!WARNING]
> Boiling udon water can foam over quickly. Never leave the pot unattended.

## Key Term

**Dashi**
: A Japanese soup stock made from kombu (kelp) and katsuobushi (bonito flakes), forming the base of most udon broths.

## Basic Ratios

| Ingredient | Amount (per serving) |
|------------|----------------------|
| Udon noodles | 200 g |
| Dashi | 400 ml |
| Soy sauce | 1 tbsp |
| Mirin | 1 tbsp |

Boil the noodles for 8–10 minutes, then rinse briefly in cold water[^1].

See also: [Making Dashi from Scratch](./making-dashi.md)

[^1]: Rinsing removes surface starch and keeps the noodles from turning gummy.
```
