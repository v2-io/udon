# Cooking Udon at Home

> [!warning] Boil-over hazard
> Udon foam and boil over quickly. Use a tall pot and stay nearby while the noodles cook.

Udon are thick wheat noodles. Boil them until tender, then rinse in cold water to stop the cooking.[^1]

## Key Terms

**Dashi**: a basic Japanese stock made by steeping kombu (kelp) and dried bonito flakes in water. It is the broth base for most udon dishes.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Udon noodles | Chewy main body |
| Dashi | Savory broth base |

Tip: ==salt the cooking water lightly== for better noodle flavor.

See also [[Dashi Stock Basics]] for the full broth recipe.

[^1]: Cook times vary by brand. Check the package and taste a strand near the end.
