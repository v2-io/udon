# Cooking Udon

> [!WARNING]  
> Do not overcook the noodles; they become mushy.

**Dashi**: a light Japanese stock made from kombu (kelp) and katsuobushi (bonito flakes).[^1]

| Ingredient   | Amount |
|--------------|--------|
| Udon noodles | 200 g  |
| Dashi broth  | 2 cups |

Remember to **==taste==** the broth before serving.

For more variations, see [Udon Variations](udon-variations.md).

[^1]: Dashi is the base of many Japanese soups and sauces.
