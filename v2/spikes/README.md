# v2/spikes — orientation notes + index

You're arriving at the ideation/spike layer of UDON's demand-first v2 effort. This file was written by the agents who came before you, for you — and your judgment about your own context budget and reading order may well exceed ours, since you can see the task in front of you and we couldn't. What follows is what we found, what it cost, where we got burned, and why each piece mattered to us; the shape it suggests is a starting point, and what serves the project outranks conforming to this file.

The one measurement worth having before you choose: a full front-to-back read of everything listed here cost about two-thirds of a working context (2026-07-29). That agent found the depth genuinely worth it *once* — it produced the synthesis below — and also found that most of it would have served fine as reference-on-demand. So this file now separates *what we'd hate to work here without* from *what we go back to when a thread demands it*. Where you draw that line is yours.

## What we'd hate to work here without

1. **[`../current-0.9.1-spec/`](../current-0.9.1-spec/)** — the language itself; nine files, ~1,500 lines, ~90 minutes whole. Joseph, on the record: "I don't think I'll ever feel comfortable with an agent who hasn't gone over the 0.9.1 spec" — this project is, at its core, *that*, and his experience with sessions that skipped it is that they went "100x worse." A natural order that worked: TUTORIAL → CORE (Appendix A first) → MODEL → the rest. Status per **C8**: semi-frozen, spec-only. Two humbling findings from agents who *had* read it whole, offered so you can beat them: reading bought comprehension but not conformant generation — a whole-spec-fresh agent still emitted plausible non-canonical constructs the same day (envelopes as element bodies, phase violations), the same imitation-not-validation gap the vivarium field report saw at low exposure — so we've taken to checking any UDON we write against the parser (`cd ../../core && cargo run --example stdin_parse`) or the highlighter rather than trusting recall; and current grammar kept sneaking into design arguments as if it were a verdict — 0.9-era spelling decisions are facts to price, and treating them as constraints on open questions is the calcification this whole layer exists to escape (O14; the glob "Never" was one such, caught 2026-07-29).
2. **[`../DECISIONS.md`](../DECISIONS.md) + [`../OPEN.md`](../OPEN.md)** — the banners and the structure, ~15 minutes. What they carry that we kept needing: the authority is two-tiered (rulings whose prose states a motivation, versus reason-less CHANGELOG-era carries — Joseph has said plainly he lacks confidence the latter reflect more than implementation convenience, and treats them as recheckable); and OPEN's own banner, his words, explains why its rows are guidance surface for the territory work rather than a ruling queue. Proposals aren't ratifications here; Joseph alone closes questions — a rule with a long incident history behind it.
3. **The register rules**, ~5 minutes: the header comments of [`schema-review/DISCUSSION-THOUGHTS.udon`](schema-review/DISCUSSION-THOUGHTS.udon) and [`../FOR-JOSEPH.udon`](../FOR-JOSEPH.udon). Short, and the cost of not holding them was real — O-items were once laundered into "steward marks" by the mere form of the ledger they were recorded in, which is the exact failure O3 describes, committed by the act of recording it.
4. **The theory's ten claims** — `../udon-needs/02-tooling-needs/reports/theory-of-agentic-tooling.md` **§0**, ~10 minutes. The design discussions here stand on these constantly — why sharp typed errors matter (bias bounded by observation ambiguity, and ambiguity is the designer's knob), why durable agent-written formats are existential rather than convenient (cross-session persistence exists only through the reinjection channel — an exact result), why refusal design is language design, why interface latency is a persistence question. Without §0, these arrive as house jargon and you'd have no way to know a deeper reason was in play or where to check it; each claim's full treatment is one hop away in the same file. Two related things we learned the hard way: the vocabulary (M_t, δ, η\*, κ×A) has precise, tier-labeled meanings, and decorative use of it reads as borrowed authority; when a theory result is load-bearing, citing the segment at its stated tier is what has survived audit — a full day of "TST grounds this" without segments did not.
5. **[`working-synopsis.md`](working-synopsis.md)**, ~25 minutes — the current big-picture carrier: what UDON could be for agents, doc-stores-as-db with the layered write-governance picture, the paths usage-vector circuit, the arity/expectation axes with their AAT grounding, extraction-first schemas, dialects-as-vocabulary. It's the cheapest way to inherit the present thinking, and it asks to be held at its own register: *proposed throughout, one mind's leans*, evolving in place (its git history shows freshness); its footnotes route into everything below, and no primary defers to it. If something more refined has replaced it since 2026-07-29, that's the better read, and this line would appreciate updating.
6. **The lessons section at the bottom**, ~2 minutes — other agents' expensive mistakes, so they can be yours for free.

## The rest — an index we return to, rather than a syllabus

A word on how this half has actually functioned: the 2026-07-28 session built it as a flat onboarding list, and the next agent — reading the courtesy glosses below — came away *feeling* oriented while having read almost nothing, which detonated mid-collaboration exactly the way assumed knowledge does. The glosses are hole-markers: a summary line is positive evidence you do *not* hold the content, and this estate has repeatedly caught grep-and-window reconstruction manufacturing confident false versions of skipped reads. The instrument that caught it was mechanical and cheap — an explicit whole/partial/unread accounting against this list — and it worked where two written warnings hadn't. Also worth carrying: the prior syntheses below are inputs, not authorities; a fully-oriented agent still over-weighted them against its own primary reads until called on it, and independent analysis that conflicts with a recorded synthesis is exactly the kind of thing worth surfacing (three-way, no verdict) rather than deferring on.

## Lessons paid for here (what they cost, and what's worked instead)

- **Deliverable register.** What earned Joseph's trust, across five authors in one day: conclusions in the first screen, certainty carried in the verb, corpus-alignment at aside weight, one named spine visible. What he caught repeatedly: connective prose borrowing measured cells' authority for unmeasured conclusions; superlative ranking; transcription acts conferring authority the content doesn't claim.
- **Divergences.** When grammar, parser, and CORE disagree, the exposition that has served is three-way and factual — what each does, with cites — because which resolution applies (backport / fix / evolve) is a spec-reasoning call that agent verdicts have gotten wrong.
- **Grammar vs design.** Present grammar is a fact to price, never the verdict on an open design question (O14). Twice in one day a syntax-era decision was found quietly swinging a design lean; wherever you find a prohibition or lean resting on spelling-incumbency, opening it has so far been more valuable than inheriting it.
- **Written UDON.** Ours drifted from law within hours of reading the law, and others imitate what lands in the corpus — incidental choices included (O11, O6). The parser is cheap and recall isn't; checking before it propagates has been the difference.
- **Prior syntheses.** Agreement within this single-author estate is coherence, not corroboration — and that applies to our own artifacts too, this file included.
- **Deaths-visible is register-scoped.** Audit/spike artifacts rightly keep their refuted claims visible with the refutation in place — there, provenance *is* the content (acid-for-corpora is the worked example, four claims killed on the page). But the convention must not travel: when spike material migrates into seeds or canon, the refuted claim is deleted and the death moves to the history layer, per integration-is-replacement. Same discipline, opposite executions, keyed by the artifact's register. (The acid agent's own parting caution, 2026-07-29, left so the worked example doesn't get over-applied.) And the third disposition completes it (Joseph, same day, the ASF practice): push each death toward a **no-go** wherever possible — a demotion worked into "no X can Y, because Z" stops being history and becomes present-truth content, canon-eligible on its own strength, often more informative about the problem than the claim it killed. The day's own specimens: the quantifier refutation localizes where corruption lives; the dead rename-guess derives O4's coexistence requirement; the learned-breakpoints negative localizes declaredness as chunking's active ingredient.
