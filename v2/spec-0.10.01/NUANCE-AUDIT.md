## The strains — reported, unresolved

1. **The framed ` ; ` terminating an open value.** The annotation — defined as contributing *nothing* — participates in value grammar as a terminator. Defensible (an annotation must be able to *begin* somewhere, and beginning ends what precedes it), but the frame would be cleaner if annotation-start were structurally identical to any marker-start; instead it has its own frame rules distinct from every guard. Held as the price of trailing annotations (`|li Item ; TODO`), explicitly a convenience purchase.
2. **Fences.** Byte-exactness is a real promise no other capture geometry makes (no dedent — even the *geometry* is content). The family holds only by letting one member opt out of the family's one shared behavior. Alternative — fences as their own material kind ("held bytes") outside captures — was rejected here for family-count economy, not principle. Genuinely unresolved.
3. **`@` and `!` sameline guard asymmetry with `<`.** References and generators open in the scan by sigil+guard; block captures need `ident:` after `<` to avoid colliding with comparison-flavored prose in value positions (`a<b`). The guard is honest but the capture's block form is the one deferred spelling that needs a two-token guard — a lexical wrinkle the "one family per species" principle didn't predict.
4. **The construed species remains unadjudicated** (redux §2.1's parked third): captures are here treated as references-into-a-vocabulary-scope for dispatch purposes, but a capture's *body* is not a head, and "construe" is not obviously resolve. The unification of dispatch (§9.3) is real; the deeper species question stays open at def/ (def-generator working notes).
