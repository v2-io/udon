## Where this got to, and what changed about using it (2026-07-29, close of the second session)

**The destination now exists: [`../theory/`](../theory/).** Until today the spike layer was both the frontier and the only home for what it found. There is now a corpus whose job is to hold the claims — one per file, ordering in `OUTLINE.udon`, conventions and gates in `FORMAT.md` (which is also the auto-loaded `CLAUDE.md`/`AGENTS.md` there). That changes this directory's role rather than its contents: **the spikes become sources, and `theory/` becomes where a source's claims get integrated.** Its outline carries 150 claim-slots and 17 named gaps across 10 parts, every row a gap — no segment has been written — and every row carries a `:see` pointing back here, section-precise, so material can be integrated rather than re-derived.

Two things about that outline worth knowing before working from it, because both were expensive to learn:

- **No row carries a `:status`, deliberately.** Strength is assigned by whoever reads the source at the primary and writes the segment; a status assigned from an index row is a proxy wearing verification's clothes. What rows *do* carry is `:max-attainable` — the ceiling, which follows from the *kind* of claim rather than from work done, and is therefore assignable in advance. A ceiling beside an absent status is a route: it says what could be established at a slot and that nobody has established it.
- **The ceilings say where the theorem-level work actually is.** 22 slots across the algebra and its consequences have `exact` ceilings and no derivation exists behind any of them; Part I is bounded at `discussion-grade` by the tier of the source it transmits from; the consequences part is `decided` throughout by construction. That distribution, not any claim in it, is the most useful thing the outline currently holds.

**A hazard specific to adding to this file.** The lessons section at the bottom is the exact channel by which a wrong instruction propagated once already — "parser-check your work" was recorded there as house wisdom and was inherited by every oriented agent until Joseph caught it (see the floor item on the old parser). Anything added here is read as settled by agents who have no way to weigh it. So what follows is state and direction, not guidance; the conventions live in `../theory/FORMAT.md`, where they can be argued with.

### Where it's headed

Named so nothing here reads as finished:

- **The outline's rows want re-sorting into three honest states.** A row that names and types a claim asserts a resolution nobody has: that a region is *exactly one* claim, of *that* kind. Where a source enumerates discretely (the map's M1–M13, the algebra's N1–N5 and R1–R4, the four fiat principles) the count is genuinely known; where continuous prose was carved into slots it is not. The proposed shape is `|segment` for the first, `|proposed` for the second — with an actual proposal *file* holding its sources, its ceiling's `:why`, and its count uncertainty — and bare `|gap` with an arc for regions where only the arc is known. Designed, not run.
- **`:max-attainable` has no `:why` yet**, which is what separates a real ceiling ("inherently empirical") from a floor on current understanding ("nobody has looked"). Roughly a third of the current ceilings are probably the second kind and should read `?`.
- **The slugs are a first pass** and have not been through a naming round. Several fail the standalone-citability criterion in `asf/doc/sop/naming.sop/principles.sop.md`, one of them a shortened form of a name ASF already flags as failing.

- **There is no mechanical check for `.udon` at all** — no canonicaliser and no conformant checker, and `fmt-md` actively corrupts UDON (assessment at `~/src/arch/utils/fmt-md/UDON-ASSESSMENT-2026-07-29.md`: the text law makes its safety premise markdown's, and joining attribute lines destroys attributes). ## Lessons paid for here (what they cost, and what's worked instead)

- **Deliverable register.** What earned Joseph's trust, across five authors in one day: conclusions in the first screen, certainty carried in the verb, corpus-alignment at aside weight, one named spine visible. What he caught repeatedly: connective prose borrowing measured cells' authority for unmeasured conclusions; superlative ranking; transcription acts conferring authority the content doesn't claim.
- **Divergences.** When grammar, parser, and CORE disagree, the exposition that has served is three-way and factual — what each does, with cites — because which resolution applies (backport / fix / evolve) is a spec-reasoning call that agent verdicts have gotten wrong.
- **Grammar vs design.** Present grammar is a fact to price, never the verdict on an open design question (O14). Twice in one day a syntax-era decision was found quietly swinging a design lean; wherever you find a prohibition or lean resting on spelling-incumbency, opening it has so far been more valuable than inheriting it.
- **Written UDON.** Ours drifted from law within hours of reading the law, and others imitate what lands in the corpus — incidental choices included (O11, O6). The parser is cheap and recall isn't; checking before it propagates has been the difference.
- **Prior syntheses.** Agreement within this single-author estate is coherence, not corroboration — and that applies to our own artifacts too, this file included.
- **Deaths-visible is register-scoped.** Audit/spike artifacts rightly keep their refuted claims visible with the refutation in place — there, provenance *is* the content (acid-for-corpora is the worked example, four claims killed on the page). But the convention must not travel: when spike material migrates into seeds or canon, the refuted claim is deleted and the death moves to the history layer, per integration-is-replacement. Same discipline, opposite executions, keyed by the artifact's register. (The acid agent's own parting caution, 2026-07-29, left so the worked example doesn't get over-applied.) And the third disposition completes it (Joseph, same day, the ASF practice): push each death toward a **no-go** wherever possible — a demotion worked into "no X can Y, because Z" stops being history and becomes present-truth content, canon-eligible on its own strength, often more informative about the problem than the claim it killed. The day's own specimens: the quantifier refutation localizes where corruption lives; the dead rename-guess derives O4's coexistence requirement; the learned-breakpoints negative localizes declaredness as chunking's active ingredient.
