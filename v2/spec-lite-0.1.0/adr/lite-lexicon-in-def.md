# Lite's terms are definition records in def/; the lexicon is generated from them

## Context and Problem Statement

On 2026-09-29 Joseph asked for a single `spec-lite-0.1.0/lexicon.md`. A first draft was written that night and deleted because it was "really sloppy", so that it "could get done with a little more thoughtfulness and rigor": the form was set aside to be redone, not abandoned. Meanwhile the SOP store set up definition records in `def/`, one term-group per file, with a generated lexicon view, which he agreed to there ([[sop/decision:lexicon-in-def]]). His 2026-09-29 single-file call was never written as a record. This record replaces it for lite.

## Decision Drivers

* One mechanism for every record: a term resolves as `[[def:slug]]` like anything else, and a definition can be revised, checked and cited on its own.
* The lexicon a reader sees can't drift from the definitions if it is generated from them.

## Assumptions

* A generator for the lexicon view will be written (`bin/`), as the addressing theory has one (`references/bin/compose-defs`). (**recorded**: the deciding agent's, at decision time)

## Considered Options

* A single hand-written `lexicon.md` (his 2026-09-29 call).
* Definition records in `def/`, with a lexicon view generated by a script from them (chosen; Joseph's lean).

## Decision Outcome

Chosen: lite's terms live as definition records in `def/`, one term-group per record. Any lexicon document is a view generated from them by a script, and is never edited by hand. Lite's terms that come from the addressing theory are cited there, not restated ([[decision:references-vocabulary]]).

### Quote

> lexicon being generated by a script that looks at the definitions in the def/** files was my lean but I am happy to be persuaded otherwise by you if you like.
>
> — Joseph, 2026-10-01 (`.int/STEWARD-VERBATIM.md`)

*The call is the udon-team agent's, which agrees with his lean and found nothing to argue against it; the Outcome is that agent's own text. His stated lean, given before the call and inviting the agent to decide, is support for it: `supported`.*

### Positive Consequences

* Definitions and the lexicon can't disagree.

### Negative Consequences

* No readable lexicon exists until the generator is written.

### What changes

* `.int/README.md`'s references to `lexicon.md` are replaced.

## Reopen when

* A single-file lexicon proves easier to keep honest than records plus a generator.

*Reopening means a new decision that supersedes this one. This outcome is never edited in place.*
