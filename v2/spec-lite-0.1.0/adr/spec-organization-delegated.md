# The organization of lite's spec is the udon-team agent's call; the 2026-09-30 skeleton is not binding

## Context and Problem Statement

`main.outline.md` carried an eleven-part, ~45-row skeleton drafted from the SOP side. Joseph asked whether the spec needs that many parts and rows; from a brief glance he suspected the skeleton was too large and not organized on principles of a spec or of UDON.

## Decision Drivers

* The spec should be as small as it needs to be, and organized on principle (Joseph, under the Quote).
* The organizer should become the domain expert and co-owner.

## Assumptions

* None recorded.

## Considered Options

* Only one option was on the table.

## Decision Outcome

Chosen: the udon-team agent decides lite's spec organization. Joseph asks it to watch for signs that a different organization is wanted rather than pushing to finish, and to launch a fork for a reorganization spike when it senses one. The existing skeleton is a starting example only.

### Quote

> Please don't feel somehow attached to the 9-section forty-five segment random stuff that the verisectorium agent put in place as an example without knowing anything really about udon. *You* are supposed to become the deep domain expert on this and co-owner, not him. […] (a) too much / much bigger than it needed to be for the official spec (I mean, look at jsons...), and (b) that it also wasn't necessarily organized in a principled way for a spec or for udon.

> I will defer to you on the organization of the spec-- I *would* ask you to not feel so much like getting it "done" that you miss signs that it might want a different organization. Please watch for such indications / gut feels -- and launch a fork to do a reorg spike and come back with its solid opinion.
>
> — Joseph, 2026-10-08 (`.int/STEWARD-VERBATIM.md`)

*His words: "I will defer to you". A grant in his own words, so `steward`. Organization decisions made under it cite this record and carry `decided-by: delegated`.*

### Positive Consequences

* The spec can be shaped by the tree it specifies.

### Negative Consequences

* None identified.

### What changes

* `main.outline.md` is to be replaced (the coordinator's next work).
