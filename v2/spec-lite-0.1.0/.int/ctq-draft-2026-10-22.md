# Critical-to-quality factors for lite (draft)

*For scoring branches of the type-and-notation exploration (`types-ideation-2026-10-22.md`). Agents are the principal readers and writers. The 2011 column is Joseph's; the "Proposed" weights are a draft for his. Nothing here is ratified.*

**How a branch is scored.** A **veto** factor is pass/fail; a branch that fails one stops. The rest are weighted 0–10 and traded against each other. A branch's **impact radius** (below) is a cost, not a quality of the language.

## A. Reading and writing (2011: Beauty, "Highest")

| | Factor | 2011 | Proposed |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Least surprise**: from a little exposure, agents guess the rest, reading and writing[^a1] | readability 10, learnability 10 | 10 |
| A2 | **Scannable** in plain text, without highlighting | Beauty (visual) | 9 |
| A3 | **Domain-forward**: the domain shows, not the plumbing[^a3] | self-description 10 | 9 |
| A3b | **Explicit self-description**, where no convention applies[^a3b] | self-description 10 | 4 |
| A4 | **Grammar simplicity**[^a4] | syntactic simplicity 8 | 8 |
| A5 | **Spec time-to-comprehension**[^a5] | — | 9 |
| A6 | **Conceptual coherence**: one reading per construct, few exceptions | Beauty (conceptual) | 8 |
| A7 | **Conciseness**, in tokens | 6 | 6 |
| A8 | **Homoiconicity** | 5 | 4 |
| — | Algorithmic declaration | 3 | 0 in lite |
| — | Turing completeness | 0 | 0: not a goal |

## Evaluating a branch, beyond the factors

- **Impact radius**: the cards a branch changes, adds or deletes, weighted by each card's `strength` (forced > decided > lean > provisional), plus whatever it reopens.
- **Pruning**: a branch stops when it fails a veto, or when it can no longer render the composite example set honestly.

[^a1]: Measured by exposure curves: fresh panels (other model families included) see a few idiomatic and a few rarer forms, then read held-out UDON into trees and write UDON from trees given as JSON, at several exposure levels. Silent misreads weigh more than errors.
[^a3]: What values are comes from convention and context (file and schema naming, the logical store, slot vocabularies), as OCaml infers strong types, not from per-value declarations; explicit marks only where they disambiguate. Joseph: "I like the *domain*'s mental model to jump out wherever possible, not the plumbing between the domain and the intermediate language or machine." Measured as plumbing per domain fact: the marks the composite set still needs once conventions are in place.
[^a3b]: Kept low: weighted high, it pulls every branch toward config and explicitness over convention.
[^a4]: Grammar size, choice points and longest holdback, checked by an interpreter that reads the grammar text.
[^a5]: How short, coherent and easy to apply the spine is. Measured by a fresh agent given only the spine answering held-out questions. Correlated with A1 and A4, not the same.
[^b1]: Every construct's kind is known within a few characters; nothing holds an unbounded stretch before deciding; lite documents parse the same under a streaming full parser.
[^c1]: The portable core (nil, booleans, strings, numbers) parses "without extra effort" into every host; a host mapping guide covers the edges (integers past 2^53, NaN, nil versus absent).
[^c5]: Generic and host-specific; lite carries claims, not meanings, and handles unknown ones predictably.
[^c7]: Cardinality by strict getters; attachments (comments, meta) change no arity.
[^d2]: A notation that needs highlighting fails A2.
