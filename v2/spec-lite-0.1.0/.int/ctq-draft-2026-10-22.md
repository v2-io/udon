# Critical-to-quality factors for lite (draft)

*For scoring branches of the type-and-notation exploration (`types-ideation-2026-10-22.md`). Agents are the principal readers and writers. The 2011 column is Joseph's; the "Proposed" weights are a draft for his. Nothing here is ratified.*

**How a branch is scored.** A **veto** factor is pass/fail (two: bounded streaming, reserve-don't-ignore); a branch that fails one stops. The rest are weighted 0–10 and traded against each other. A branch's **impact radius** (below) is a cost, not a quality of the language.

## A. Reading and writing (2011: Beauty, visual and conceptual, "Highest")

| | Factor | 2011 | Proposed | Measure | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Least surprise.** Having seen a few idiomatic forms and a few rarer ones, an agent correctly guesses the rest, reading (UDON → tree) and writing (tree → UDON) | readability 10, learnability 10 | 10 | exposure curves from fresh panels, other model families included; silent misreads weigh more than errors | |
| A2 | **Scannable in plain text**, without highlighting; looks like what it means | Beauty (visual): Highest | 9 | panel time and accuracy on unhighlighted pages | |
| A3 | **Domain-forward.** The domain's mental model shows; what values are comes from convention and context (file and schema naming, the logical store, slot vocabularies), as OCaml infers strong types, not from per-value declarations. Explicit marks only where they disambiguate | self-description 10 | 9 | plumbing per domain fact: marks and annotations the composite set needs once conventions are in place | "I like the *domain*'s mental model to jump out wherever possible, not the plumbing between the domain and the intermediate language or machine" (Joseph) |
| A3b | Explicit self-description, where a document has no convention to lean on | self-description 10 | 4 | — | kept low: weighted high, it pulls every branch toward config and explicitness over convention |
| A4 | **Grammar simplicity** | syntactic simplicity 8 | 8 | grammar size, choice points, longest holdback (checked by an interpreter that reads the grammar text) | |

## Evaluating a branch, beyond the factors

- **Impact radius**: the cards a branch changes, adds or deletes, weighted by each card's `strength` (forced > decided > lean > provisional), plus whatever it reopens. A cost, set against the factor scores.
- **Pruning**: a branch stops when it fails a veto, or when it can no longer render the composite example set honestly.
