# Critical-to-quality factors for lite (draft)

*For scoring branches of the type-and-notation exploration (`types-ideation-2026-10-22.md`). Agents are the principal readers and writers. The 2011 column is Joseph's; the priorities are a draft for his. Nothing here is ratified. Within each group, rows are in priority order.*

**How a branch is scored.** A **veto** factor is pass/fail; a branch that fails one stops. The rest are weighted 0–10 and traded against each other. A branch's **impact radius** (below) is a cost, not a quality of the language.

## A. Reading, writing and learning (2011: Beauty group)

| | Factor | 2011 | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | **Beauty**, visual and conceptual[^a1] | Beauty: Highest | highest |
| A2 | **Least surprise**: from a little exposure, agents guess the rest, reading and writing[^a2] | readability 10, learnability 10 | 10 |
| A3 | **Spec clarity**: time to comprehend and apply the spec's spine[^a3] | — | 9 |
| A4 | **Syntactic simplicity**[^a4] | syntactic simplicity 8 | 8 |

Not goals in lite: homoiconicity (2011: 5), algorithmic declaration (3), Turing completeness (0).

## B. Performance and robustness (2011: Performance, "Very High")

| | Factor | 2011 | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 | **Bounded streaming**[^b1] | parse speed 9, throughput 10 | veto |
| B2 | **Reserve, don't ignore**: lite refuses what a later UDON may define | — | veto |
| B3 | **Stability**: same tree from every parser; errors, not guesses | 10 | 10 |
| B4 | **Parse and generate speed** | 9 | 8 |
| B5 | **Size**: bytes, and tokens for agents | document size 7, conciseness 6 | 6 |
| B6 | Transport speed | 5 | 2 |

## C. Utility (2011: Utility, "Very High")

**What lite can carry**

| | Factor | 2011 | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Structured data**, as JSON and YAML are used[^c1] | declarative data 10, hierarchy 10, serialization 7 | 10 |
| C2 | **XML/HTML**, mixed content included | document mixing 9 | 10 |
| C3 | **Markdown in prose stays Markdown** | narrative texts 5 | 9 |
| C4 | **Other languages**, exactly (fences) | language mixing 9 | 8 |
| C5 | **Units and quantities** | — | 7 |

**How lite fits the systems around it**

| | Factor | 2011 | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| C6 | **Extensibility**: dialects, notations, type extensions[^c6] | 8 | 8 |
| C7 | **Schemas and slot types**, aliases, domain vocabularies | in-doc 7, standard 7 | 8 |
| C8 | **Predictable tree and API**[^c8] | standard API 8, in-doc API 8 | 8 |
| C9 | **Round-trip fidelity**: content, meta, ornament[^c9] | — | 7 |
| C10 | **Document composition**: partials, records, stores | document mixing 9 | 6 |

Not goals in lite: universal templating (2011: 9), relational data (7). Online processing (6) is B1. Specialized serialization, document universality and namespaced type mixing (5, 5, 4) return under C6 and C7.

## Evaluating a branch, beyond the factors

- **Impact radius**: the cards a branch changes, adds or deletes, weighted by each card's `strength` (forced > decided > lean > provisional), plus whatever it reopens.
- **Pruning**: a branch stops when it fails a veto, or when it can no longer render the composite example set honestly.

[^a1]: Left to the reader's own sense of it, on purpose; branches may interpret it differently. Some aspects Joseph has named, as examples rather than a definition: the domain's model showing rather than the plumbing ("I like the *domain*'s mental model to jump out wherever possible, not the plumbing between the domain and the intermediate language or machine"); ornament such as vertical alignment and blank lines that speed reading of the domain information; and constraints such as meaningful indentation, which restrict layout freedom in a way that is more conceptually beautiful.
[^a2]: Measured by exposure curves: fresh panels (other model families included) see a few idiomatic and a few rarer forms, then read held-out UDON into trees and write UDON from trees given as JSON, at several exposure levels. Silent misreads weigh more than errors.
[^a3]: Measured by a fresh agent given only the spine answering held-out questions. Correlated with A2 and A4, not the same.
[^a4]: Grammar size, choice points and exceptions to a construct's one reading, checked by an interpreter that reads the grammar text.
[^b1]: Every construct's kind is known within a few characters; nothing holds an unbounded stretch before deciding; lite documents parse the same under a streaming full parser.
[^c1]: The portable core (nil, booleans, strings, numbers) parses "without extra effort" into every host; a host mapping guide covers the edges (integers past 2^53, NaN, nil versus absent).
[^c6]: Generic and host-specific; lite carries claims, not meanings, and handles unknown ones predictably.
[^c8]: Cardinality by strict getters; attachments (comments, meta) change no arity.
[^c9]: Content < content+meta < content+meta+ornament; round-trip keeps what it is asked to.
[^d2]: A notation that needs highlighting to be read is weaker on A1 and A2.
