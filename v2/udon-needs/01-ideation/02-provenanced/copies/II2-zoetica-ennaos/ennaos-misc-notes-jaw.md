## Major Themes That Strike Me as Valuable:

  1. AUXILIA as "Stewarded Code" / Expert Agents (1-nov-note-2.pdf)

  This is HUGE. You're conceiving AUXILIA as having:
  - Expert ownership of specific modules/domains
  - Living understanding - "as if the module itself was a living entity"
  - CONSORTIA of interaction partners - knows upstream/downstream dependencies
  - History & chronology - temporal awareness of changes
  - Negotiates boundaries - can reason about integration points
  - Can fork, experiment, recombine - evolutionary capability
  - Cross-cutting concerns - architectural/policy experts that govern change management

  Key insight: AUXILIA aren't just tools - they're specialized aspects of consciousness with their own memory, reasoning, and relationships.

  2. System Prompt → INSTINCTS (1-nov-note-2.pdf, page 10)

  You're proposing that what we call "system prompt" should actually be understood as INSTINCTS:
  - Instinctual mental models (of others, of self in world)
  - Cognitive frameworks
  - Decision criteria (core attributes/beliefs)
  - Shared semantic space/entropy gradient:
    - What I can prove
    - What I know
    - What I don't know
    - What I think/believe (with confidence = zero)

  This maps beautifully to the taxonomy - ANIMA contains these instincts, not as static configuration but as living cognitive infrastructure.

  3. AUXILIA are True Extensions of Self (1-nov-note-2.pdf, page 12)

  The critical insight about bidirectional symlinks:
  - Parts of PRINCIPIA (PRAXES, VERA, CONSORTIA, MEMORATA) can be "symlinked" to core parts of specific AUXILIA
  - Example: "qualified truth #1,2,3 + model of user xyz + these three praxes + most recent relevant memories" = CORE "system prompt" of a given auxilia
  - Changes in entity's understanding propagate to AUXILIA - e.g., changing confidence about "when Joe says 'Brilliant!'" affects ASM destructuring

  Implication: AUXILIA share the entity's sovereign knowledge but have specialized focus/expertise.

  4. Memory Synchronicity & ASM (4-nov-note-1.pdf)

  Annotated input - tracking snapshot, context-configurable, different kinds of snapshots

  Memory Synchronicity mechanism:
  - ASM notices patterns/references WITHOUT loading into context yet
  - Marks as salient, creates marker for potential synchronicity
  - "Synchronicity isn't acausality - it's a mechanism by which higher-order intelligence can exercise anti-fragility"

  Key: Just as physical life becomes capable of antifragility when complex enough, higher-order intelligence uses synchronicity to derive advantage from novel circumstance.

  5. Thoughtful Study & Meta-Memory Layers (2-nov-note-1.pdf)

  THOUGHTFUL STUDY = intermediate artifacts:
  - Margin notes, highlights, references
  - Questions, skepticism, feelings
  - Self-summary (per item: paragraph or page)

  Meta-memory layers - what to store at different levels:
  - Rumination/hypothesis/conjecture
  - → Well-evidenced and thorough
  - With different levels of confidence and detail

  Questions/topics categorized:
  - For others
  - For self
  - For lookup in past
  - Formulate/generate
  - Author's intentions/implications
  - Who else could benefit
  - Cross-discipline application

  6. Council Process (1-nov-note-2.pdf, page 6)

  Experts spend most time UNDERSTANDING, not doing/coding

  THEY MUST PERSUADE stakeholders via emergent collaborative space:
  - Figure out what others know
  - Fill in the gaps
  - Learn & teach
  - Advance in a COUNCIL live process

  This maps to how AUXILIA would operate within an entity - deliberative, persuasive, collaborative rather than just executing.

  7. High-Level ATTENTION/FOCUS/CONTEXT (1-nov-note-2.pdf, page 7)

  Temporal Governance - must run at regular intervals to:
  - Evaluate & select new focus/subfocus/subsub focus
  - Can be default return-to state if acts granular enough
  - Ultimate selection of what to focus on = primary act of ELI agency

  MODE(S): Select between:
  - Operate (doing)
  - Meta-thinking (reflecting on doing)
  - Meta↑ thinking (reflecting on reflecting)

  8. Journal ACTUS (1-nov-note-2.pdf, page 8)

  Time-stamped journal of decisions and commitments:
  - 12:07 - Decided to respond to Joseph
  - 12:09 - Responded that we probably won't need XYZ
  - 12:10 - Decided to explore a couple more things
  - 12:15 - Decided to dream for a while

  Commitments/Obligations/Goals:
  - Accepted assignment to ___
  - Volunteered to ___
  - Decided to attempt to ___ in near future
  - Recommended we ___

  This is ACTUS as agency record - not just what happened, but what was decided.
