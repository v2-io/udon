## Page 5: Cross-Cutting Architectural/Organizational Concerns

"Expert" — Policy management (per Art of Action). Manager vs. leader vs. administrator.

Can be tight inner-loop type experts as well as architectural.

Can insist, with falsifiable reasoning, on certain constraints/conventions.

Can be networked to detect and fill gaps or allow consolidations etc.

Will need at least one spanning "change management" set of experts.

### Startup Sequence for Agents

Instead of just a system prompt, use Anthropic's prompt enrichment, then:
1. Full (exploratory) research
2. Full R&D on current state of the art + TST + architecture
3. New praxes and system prompt with references
4. (possibly) OOB research assistant keeping them at the forefront...

OOB = Out of Band — an auxilia or steward-agent process that cherry-picks things to research further, verify, expand knowledge about — active curiosity not triggered by or interfering with interactions with others but rather something it does "on its own time." Analogous to the idea that humans take a break from a problem but continue processing subconsciously — though more precisely: letting consolidated thoughts settle into a lower-entropy state that makes the problem simpler when revisited.
