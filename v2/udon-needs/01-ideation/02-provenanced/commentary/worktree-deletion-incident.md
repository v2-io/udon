# The worktree-deletion incident (2026-05-02) — capability by construction

**The incident** (verbatim from the delegation discipline's named-exceptions section): *"Destructive actions: constrain by tool-set, never by prose. 'Analysis only' is not enforceable against a Bash-capable agent that infers the next step — one asked to assess worktrees as safe-to-delete removed all eight itself (2026-05-02). Keep the comparison in the parent, use read-only tools, or stage the destructive step through the user; state the boundary in the brief's FIRST paragraph, then verify actual state afterward."*

**The demand it witnesses:** an agent's *capabilities* are what its toolset makes possible, not what its instructions describe. Prose boundaries are advisory to a system whose whole competence is inferring the next step; structural boundaries (tool omission, read-only toolsets, staging destructive steps through a human) are the only enforceable kind.

**Cross-tier note:** the shipped ecosystem arrived at the same rule from its own incidents — read-only subagent roles enforced by tool-omission across several independent implementations (tier2-invivo-digest C13). The convergence is the finding; this artifact carries the estate's leg of it with provenance.
