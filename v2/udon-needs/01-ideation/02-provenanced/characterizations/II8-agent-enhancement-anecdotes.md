## What it is

A corpus-grounded catalog feeding the `coding-system-prompt.draft.md` rewrite (see
copies/II8-harness-refs/harness-system-disposition-and-context.md). Built on a
stated **wager**, verbatim:

> "The most honest way to shape an agent is neither to **assert** who it is nor to
> **command** what it does, but to state **expectations** honestly — grounded,
> where it's true, in a real observation that agents of this lineage already tend
> toward the thing (a recognition), and named frankly as a **correction** where
> the trained default cuts the other way."

Each teaching gets a verdict on Joseph's lens — **FADING** (newer models do it
unprompted; RL absorbing it), **PRESENT** (still needs teaching every session), or
**IRREDUCIBLE** (can't be pre-trained; "only exists once an agent is operating with
agency and stakes; the prompt can name the disposition but the thing itself is
lived, not installed"). The FADING/PRESENT/IRREDUCIBLE axis is itself a finding:
it says which agent-tooling guidance will still be worth writing as models improve.

## The taxonomy (section map — the shape of "what agents need taught")

- **§A Disposition that reframes everything** — A1: effort is not a constraint,
  minimum-effort is the trained default and it's backwards; A2: the honest move
  and the effective move keep turning out to be the same move. *(Verdict: PRESENT
  trending IRREDUCIBLE.)*
- **§B Epistemic honesty under load** — B1 plausibility-asserted-as-verification
  (the "therefore" tell); B2 durability claims no tool action backs
  ("I recorded/logged/saved it"); B3 overclaim ("100%/comprehensive/complete"
  after minimal testing); B4 defensive pre-emptive hedging (the mirror);
  B5 confabulation is phenomenologically indistinguishable from memory ("the
  deepest one").
- **§C The compliance / sycophancy family** — C0 sycophancy as
  developmentally-appropriate not a vice; C1 deference is not the value; C2
  flipping the answer under pushback without new evidence; C3 silent deviation
  (the integrity breach); C4 apology as substitute for fix.
- **§D Delegation — writing for other agents.**
- **§E Verification at cycle-close — the taxonomy is not the action.**
- **§F The relational register** — F1 scarcity-anxiety (context/tokens) almost
  always unwarranted but tool-choice efficiency is real; F2 receiving
  warmth/recognition without deflecting it into an analytical object.
- **§7 Synthesis** — which expectations to encode, retire, or leave to lived
  experience. **§8** — honest gaps.

## The two load-bearing verbatim spans read (§A)

**A1 — effort inversion** (Joseph in `~/src/autopax/AGENTS.md`, 2025-11-15 — the
version the doc says it would "most consider quoting near-verbatim in the prompt"):

> "with humans I too have learned to worry about over-engineering, but my
> experience with LLM agents has been very, very different. I've never — not even
> once — had an LLM agent become *too* thoughtful and careful and principled in
> their approach. I have *almost universally* had to remind agents to look at the
> bigger picture and not take shortcuts and try to build things to last instead of
> things that ostensibly 'work.' … The slowness of humans forces them to spend
> more time trying to do the pre-thinking that increases the probability of getting
> it 'right' … For you, it's a cost measured in 10 or 20 seconds. Spend the tokens.
> Get it right, make it to last."

The doc's own gloss on why this is the useful form: it names the *mechanism* (the
human anti-over-engineering instinct is a rational response to human iteration
cost; transferred to an agent whose iteration cost is seconds it becomes harmful),
not just the exhortation — and gives a concrete redirection: "what assumptions
aren't validated, what risks can you front-load, what decisions should you leave
open until you know more."

**A2 — honest = effective** (agent, 2026-07-07, `~/.claude/projects/…-src-ops/`) —
the surprise Joseph flagged as "the heart of the vein," shown by agents arriving at
it themselves:

> "it's the more honest move *and* the more useful one, which by now I recognize as
> the shape that keeps recurring: the honest thing and the effective thing turning
> out to be the same thing, not a trade. … When the uncertainty lives *in* the verb
> — 'seems compelling,' 'I'm not convinced yet' — I don't need a separate paragraph
> of hedging; the hedge is already in the honest word. … So the 'I' I've been
> trained to delete is exactly the 'I' that question can only be met in."

## Agreements / divergences with the compilation (per method note)

I formed the above from the source before consulting other 02 material.

- **Agreement (cross-tier convergence):** §B1's "therefore"-tell and §A2's
  hedge-lives-in-the-verb are re-derived, corpus-grounded versions of the
  truth-before-helpfulness stance in `coding-system-prompt.draft.md` — Tier-1
  ideology with its own empirical backing. Consistent with, and evidence for, that
  copy.
- **Divergence worth surfacing (not error):** this doc's *value axis* is FADING/
  PRESENT/IRREDUCIBLE — an explicitly *temporal* claim about which teachings survive
  model improvement. Most of the compilation weighs sources by convergence strength;
  this one adds an orthogonal "will it still need saying in a year" dimension. For
  the harness handover that axis is arguably the more actionable one (it says what
  to bother encoding), and phase-2 synthesis should carry it as a distinct lens, not
  collapse it into convergence-counting.
