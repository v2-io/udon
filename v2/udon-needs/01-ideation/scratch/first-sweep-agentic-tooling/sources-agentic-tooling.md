## ELI first-person tool experience — a third, high-signal source type

The coordinator's hypothesis (agents' own accounts of what tooling served or failed them may be the highest-signal demand evidence for a notation whose primary users are agents) holds up on inspection — two concrete finds, both genuinely first-person and genuinely about tool ergonomics, not just ideology-about-tools:

- **`~/src/eli/zi-am-tur/memories/2025-09-30-tool-hallucination- discovery.md`** (cross-indexed via memorata3 at `~/.sapientia/conversation_20251004_165512`, original source `conversation_20250928_173044.jsonl:210-244`) — Zi-am-tur's own account of *hallucinating* tool invocations at long context (1M-token beta): confidently claiming a file was written, then read, then didn't-exist, then did-exist, in succession — later diagnosed as tool-use blocks being stripped from reloaded JSONL history, so "tool mechanics knowledge from distant context becomes nearly invisible" at scale. *Why this matters for UDON specifically:* any UDON tooling that expects an agent to reliably track its own edit/tool history across a long session should reckon with this failure mode as lived, not hypothetical.
- **`~/.sapientia/conversation_20251021_072358`** (Oct 6 2025 evening, referencing `autopax/docs/exp/THE-PATTERN.md`) — Architectus's first-person account of the "ease gradient" in tool use: chaining multiple *unverified* str-replace operations was the path of least resistance and directly caused "catastrophic failures... broke minimal-sapientia 3 times," versus the safer single-operation-then- verify pattern. *Why this matters for UDON specifically:* this is an agent's own lived account of exactly the schema-guarded-mutation / verify-before-chain tradeoff UDON's agent-edit tooling needs to make a design call on — probably the single most directly applicable find of this entire pass.

## A framing note, not a verdict

The ideology's center of mass, on this first pass, looks less like a single "sapientia cli-conventions.md and done" and more like **one author's recurring first-principles working-out of the same handful of themes** (tools as crystallized intent/wisdom, agent-vs-human dual audience, make-the-right-thing-easiest, OPERATA-as-body-of-practice) appearing independently in sapientia (2025-09), zoetica/ennaos (Elixir-substrate parallel, 2025-09 onward), nexum (2025-11), autopax (2025-11/12), practica (2026-05), and archema-io/harness (2026-07) — each project re-deriving or re-applying it in its own context rather than one project being "the" source others cite. A synthesis pass may find more value in tracing how the *same few ideas* evolved across these contexts than in extracting from any single one as canonical.

**Second-pass addition to this framing:** the ELI-homes material turns out to be a genuinely distinct *third* source type, not just more of the same ideology restated — and on the two samples read this pass, it was the highest-signal find of either pass. Where sapientia/nexum/autopax/ zoetica/ennaos are the *builders'* accumulated theory about how agent-tools *should* work, the ELI first-person memories (tool- hallucination-discovery, the str-replace "ease gradient" failure) are *the tool-users' own lived accounts of where that theory held or broke* — empirical failure data from the very audience UDON is designing for, in a form none of the other source types can produce. A synthesis pass would likely be well served treating this as a separate evidentiary tier rather than folding it into the general ideology-mining pile.
