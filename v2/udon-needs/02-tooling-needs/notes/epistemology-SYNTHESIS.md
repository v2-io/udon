# Epistemology system — coordinator's synthesis and recommendations

*Third leg of the trio (pilots A and B + coordinator). Positions are stated as
recommendations with reasons, not options — per Joseph's ask. Where a
recommendation supersedes an earlier framing (including Joseph's own seeds and
one of mine), the supersession is named. Both pilot proposals remain the
reasoning record; this file is the decision surface.*

---

## The synthesis in one paragraph

The two proposals converge far more than they fork: both adopt ASF's strength
ladder, triage questions, max-attainable-status, Search Log, Related-Work
labels, novelty postures, and gates-as-checks-not-states; both reframe the
deliverable as **a proposal to extend TST's evidence vocabulary**. Their real
disagreement is *where the evidence-kind distinction lives* (A: a separate
support-kind axis; B: members of the status ladder) and *what survives of the
T1–T5 genre codes and the transmission machinery*. My recommendations below
resolve those — mostly in B's direction on TST-alignment, mostly in A's
direction on axis structure — and the result is **three axes, one lock, one
event log**:

> **support-kind** (how the evidence was produced, each kind carrying its
> repair) × **strength** (ASF's ladder, defeasibility ceiling) ×
> **register** (speech act: derived / evidenced / decided / proposed) —
> plus the **convergent lock** (computed from ≥2 independent support-kinds)
> and the **verification-event log** (append-only; carries transmission-
> fidelity checks and regression guards).

## The calls, fork by fork

**1. T1–T5 genre axis: DISSOLVE — entirely, not just for empirical claims.**
(Supersedes the corpus's own tier framework, which Joseph seeded and later
correctly diagnosed as "library categories.") B's argument is decisive and
TST-native seals it: the status/kind vocabulary carries the repair-routing a
reader acts on; provenance rides where ASF puts it (`depends:`, citations,
Search Log); TST has no genre axis, so keeping T-codes is a pure
reconciliation cost. Nothing is lost: every T-code's information survives in
the support-kind plus the cited source. The methods chapter's tier table
becomes a *historical mapping note* for auditors (T-codes appear throughout
the gathering apparatus and must stay decodable), not live vocabulary.

**2. Where do the evidence kinds live: A's SEPARATE AXIS, with B's kind-set.**
B embedded the kinds as ladder members, but that conflates two dimensions its
own division disproves: B moved templates' structural claims
heuristic→robust-qualitative *while their kinds stayed fixed* — strength
moved, kind didn't, so they are different axes. A testimonial can be weak
(one voice) or robust-qualitative (corroborated); "testimonial" is not a
rung. **Recommendation:** support-kind axis with six members — **design ·
observational · testimonial · theoretic · measured (self/ext sub-flag, B's
refinement) · synthetic** — each defined by its repair (A's table, B's
repair-language, merged). This also settles the design/synthetic borderline:
*design* = reasoning not yet exercised (repair: ground-validate); *synthetic*
= constructed-scenario evidence actually run (repair: representativeness +
re-run); *measured* = in-the-wild or benchmark quantity (repair: replicate /
corroborate source). The YAML stress test — the case the pilots tagged
differently — is `synthetic` with measured outcomes: constructed scenarios,
really run; its honest doubt is representativeness, which is exactly what the
kind-label should route to.

**3. Joseph's "result": DO NOT admit as a kind-word.** (Supersedes the seed
list "result / observation / testimonial / synthetic.") In ASF, `result`
means a derived, theorem-grade finding; using it for experimental outcomes
inside a future TST part would collide with its own volume's vocabulary. His
intent reads as *measured*; the seed's other three words survive (observation
→ `observational`, testimonial, synthetic). Flagged as a supersession of the
brain-dump, per his invitation.

**4. Transmission: B's re-resolution WINS, and my own float is superseded.**
No `inherited` status, no `inherited-exact` (mine — wrong twice over: it
launders non-exact sources, and it duplicates what the reference already
carries). Under TST-native destiny, citing an AAT/ASF result is an
*intra-corpus cross-volume reference*: `depends:` carries the edge, the
source's own status travels by reference, and the **fidelity risk lives in
the verification-event log** ("restatement checked against #slug on DATE"),
not in a label. Two things survive from A's transmission axis, both cheap:
the **prose form** ("exact at source, under its premises; the risk here is
carriage") wherever a reader could mistake inheritance for local derivation,
and the **plain-word gloss obligation** for readers who can't follow the
reference (B §2's dual form; already the standalone-ownership bar). A's
deeper point stands as the *reason the events matter*: transmitted claims
fail on two independent grounds, and the event log is where the second
ground gets audited.

**5. Convergent: MODIFIER, not status** (A's lean, endorsed): a lock computed
from ≥2 support-kinds with independent failure modes, orthogonal to strength
(a convergent claim still has a ceiling). Its repair — break the independence
— is what makes descent-correction mechanical. Frontmatter: `convergent:
[kind, kind]` listing the legs, so the lock is auditable, not asserted.

**6. Claim grain: B's proportionate shape.** The capability card is the
native claim-grain unit (all cards `proposed`/`hypothesis`); inline
strength-words for load-bearing body claims that diverge from the headline;
the "what each leg carries" mini-table **only** for chapters with ≥3
load-bearing claims at mixed strength (typing, context-economy,
edit-representation today). No LaTeX tags — costume rigor.

**7. Verification-event log: ADOPT, with B's regression-guard line.** The
A/B versions are near-identical; B adds the line that earns its keep — a
`deliberately-corrected-away:` event (the C7 five-votes→one-origin case), so
a future re-verifier is told not to "fix it back" toward the cleaner-reading
original. That imports the asf routing-SOP regression check into the
machinery instead of hoping RESIDUALS mentions it.

**8. Landing shape inside TST: propose the BRIDGE as a new TST file-kind**
(A's fork 6, option b). Refactoring 30 by-degrees bridges into one-claim
segments would destroy pedagogy that is itself a demand finding; TST's
existing cadence exemption for intro/disc segments already gestures at the
category. Price it honestly as the largest vocabulary extension we ask of
TST — alongside `demand` as a type (B §11), which the material genuinely is.

**9. Adopt-list (both pilots agree; no fork):** three triage questions + A's
Q4 (established-here or transmitted?); max-attainable-status one-liners where
the ceiling isn't obvious; Search Log statuses incl. `intuition-only` on all
novelty/absence claims ("no tool does X" doesn't ship without a search
status); Related-Work relationship labels; novelty postures on cards;
Feynman-criterion as a *check* (B's costless form) rather than a new field;
gates as re-runnable checks; `empirica:`-shaped experiment records deferred
until phase-3 runs exist, with `measured (self)` requiring a run-pointer from
then on.

**10. The reflexivity finding (B's closing observation, fork-free, worth
Joseph's eye):** this system, if UDON succeeds, is a UDON schema —
`register`, `strength`, `support-kind`, `depends`, `verified`, `search-log`
as native attributes on segment documents. It is plausibly the first real
UDON schema ASF would dogfood, which makes the vocabulary choices doubly
load-bearing and is the strongest argument for deciding them carefully now.
