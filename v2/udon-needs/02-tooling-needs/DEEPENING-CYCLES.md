# Deepening cycles — a workflow for iterating this report toward rigor

**Status:** workflow sketch, ratified in intent by Joseph 2026-07-22 (his
design; wording here is a first draft he has not yet reviewed — improve it).
**Who reads this:** any agent (any substrate) taking a deepening assignment on
this report, and whoever coordinates a cycle. The standing license and
quality bars in [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) apply in full and are assumed
below rather than repeated.

---

## The idea

The report is end-to-end and honest, but depth arrived unevenly and its
epistemics can get much more rigorous. Joseph's proposed shape: farm the
chapters out in **small windows** to focused agents who each go *deep* on a
few — then shift the window and go again, so every seam between chapters
eventually falls in the middle of someone's assignment rather than at
everyone's edge.

One cycle, roughly:

1. Chapters are grouped into divisions of **3–5** (a rolling window over
   OUTLINE order).
2. Each division goes to one agent, who works it deeply (below).
3. When all divisions land: cross-assignment notes are distributed and
   integrated, the OUTLINE is updated from the accepted structural
   suggestions, and a coordinator (or the next cycle's agents) reconciles.
4. **Shift the window offset by one** and iterate — the boundary
   discontinuities the last cycle couldn't see from inside a division are
   now interior to someone's assignment.

Cycles repeat until reads stop finding seams — the double-round-trip
instinct applied to prose.

## What a deepening agent does with a division

You have **the same privilege and authority as the report's authors — and
then some**. Within your assigned chapters, directly: pull in additional
nuance and side-reports from the corpus, run the deep research, do the
de-novo user-voice surveying, verify claims at their sources, build rigor
and epistemic honesty into what's there, add and refine capability cards,
and strengthen the ideation. Beyond your chapters, propose freely: new
organizations of the outline itself, additional chapters, splits, better
foundational work that should come *before* your assigned territory —
whatever the report needs. The one mechanical boundary: **direct edits stay
inside your assigned chapters** — for dedicated focus and collision
protection while several agents work at once — and everything else travels
through notes (next section).

Orientation before writing (the reading that makes depth possible): the
theory survey and how the report uses ASF's quantities
([`reports/`](reports/) — the theory survey report; the methods chapter for
the register discipline); what already exists in the corpus and what
`memorata3-search` can reach (the [`../CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md) license
tells you when to reach); your chapters' `depends:` chains read in order;
and [`RESIDUALS.md`](RESIDUALS.md) for the live open items.

## The cross-notes channel

A shared directory — `notes/` — holds working-notes files keyed by chapter
slug (e.g. `notes/for-addressing-is-the-long-pole.md`, plus
`notes/for-OUTLINE.md` and `notes/general.md`). Anything you want changed
outside your division goes there: a suggested edit, a suspicion ("I wonder
if that other chapter actually covers this or not"), feedback, an idea, a
tension you noticed at your boundary. The next agent assigned to that
chapter receives its notes file as part of their assignment and disposes of
each note visibly (taken / adapted / declined-with-reason). Notes are cheap
— when in doubt, write the note. Ideation *will* generate them constantly;
that's the channel working.

## The one thing held above everything

Joseph, near-verbatim: honor **truth above all other factors**. This is a
creative work — hypotheses and risks are welcome — but it is *always honest
about its rigor*. Every claim in its true register (derived / evidenced /
decided / proposed); every hypothesis marked as one; every verification
done at the source, not from memory; every gap named rather than written
around. If deepening a chapter means discovering it was wrong, that
discovery is the work succeeding.

## First-cycle work already known

- **Epistemic-status recalibration.** The evidence "tiers" are library
  categories (provenance genres), not epistemology. ASF's claim-level
  status vocabulary (exact / conditional-with-named-premises /
  robust-qualitative / measured / heuristic / hypothesis /
  discussion-grade) is the model — see the asf corpus itself for worked
  examples of the rigor. Claims should carry claim-level strength; genre
  stays as provenance. The frontmatter machinery follows.
- The three open items in [`RESIDUALS.md`](RESIDUALS.md): the ✦→capability-
  card retrofit of the earlier chapters; landing the codex paths testimony
  beside the Gemini artifact; and the cold cross-chapter read in OUTLINE
  order — which the first cycle's agents effectively perform by working
  with fresh eyes.
